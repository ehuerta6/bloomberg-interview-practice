# 05 — Checkout Price Mismatch

**Type:** Engineering Ticket · **Difficulty:** Medium · **Target time:** 45–60 minutes

This original CIC exercise is grounded in Cappy Marketplace, not an official Bloomberg interview question.

## Pricing contract for this ticket

The unit price is quoted when a product is first added to a cart. That quote remains attached to the cart line while its quantity changes. A catalog price update does not silently reprice an existing line. Removing and adding the product again creates a line at the then-current catalog price. A completed order stores the cart's quoted unit price, and its order total equals the sum of its persisted line totals.

This contract is intentional for this exercise and takes precedence over the generic current-catalog-price description elsewhere in the Marketplace guide.

## Ticket

A shopper adds products to a cart. Later, an admin changes one product's catalog price. The cart and checkout can then disagree with the price the shopper was shown when the item was added, and the persisted order may not reflect the agreed quote.

Trace where prices are read, calculated, and persisted across cart updates, cart retrieval, checkout, and order snapshots. Preserve integer-cent calculations and unrelated inventory behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- A price change after add does not alter the existing cart line's quoted unit price or displayed total.
- Increasing or setting that line's quantity preserves its quote.
- Removing and re-adding the product uses the new catalog price.
- Checkout charges and persists the quoted unit price, with `total_cents` equal to the sum of `line_total_cents`.
- The behavior is covered by focused tests for both cart display and persisted order snapshots.
