# 05 — Checkout Price Mismatch

**Type:** Engineering ticket · **Difficulty:** Medium · **Target time:** 45–60 minutes

## Ticket

A shopper adds an item to a cart, then an administrator changes its catalog price. The shopper's cart and the completed order may show different amounts.

The Marketplace guide says cart totals use current catalog prices and that order lines retain the unit price recorded at purchase. It does not specify what the cart should show after a catalog price changes. Confirm the intended behavior for existing cart lines and checkout before changing the implementation.

Once that product decision is clear, trace how prices and totals are calculated and persisted through cart updates, cart retrieval, and checkout. Keep calculations in integer cents and preserve unrelated inventory behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- Document the agreed behavior for an existing line after a catalog price change, including quantity updates and removing then re-adding the item.
- Cart display and checkout follow that behavior consistently.
- Persisted order lines retain the unit price charged, and `total_cents` equals the sum of `line_total_cents`.
- Focused tests cover cart display and persisted order snapshots.
