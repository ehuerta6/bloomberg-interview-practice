# 04 — Cart Ownership Leak

**Type:** PR Review · **Difficulty:** Medium–Hard · **Target time:** 30–45 minutes

This original CIC exercise uses fictional local shoppers only. The local `X-User-Id` selector is a practice identity, not a real credential.

## Scenario

A cart retrieval refactor adds lookup by cart identifier. The happy path works for the selected shopper, but the new lookup does not consistently bind the requested cart to the authenticated shopper. A shopper who learns another local cart ID may be able to read or change that cart.

## Review deliverable

Review the exact proposed diff and its tests. Report actionable findings with file/line, triggering request, violated ownership contract, and impact. Recommend focused tests for a shopper requesting and modifying another shopper's cart, plus the ordinary owner and admin cases. Explain what the included tests establish and leave unproven. A replacement implementation is optional.

Use two seeded or test-created mock shoppers and local sample data. Do not use personal data, external services, or real credentials. Do not infer that a passing owner request proves the cross-shopper boundary.
