21 · Retry Backoff Planner

PR: Validate and cap retry delays

Summary

A client library plans delays between attempts after a temporary failure. Review whether the schedule respects its retry count, multiplier, and configured ceiling.

Input and output

retry_delays(max_retries, base_delay, multiplier=2, max_delay=None) returns a list of wait durations, one for each retry after the initial request.

Expected behavior and invariants

  • The first wait is base_delay; each subsequent wait multiplies the prior wait by multiplier.
  • When supplied, max_delay caps every wait, including the first.
  • Retry count must be a nonnegative integer; settings must be numeric and nonnegative, with multiplier at least 1. Booleans are not numeric settings.
  • A zero retry count returns an empty list.

Review task

Trace the sequence before and after the cap engages. Check fractional inputs, zero retries, and invalid booleans or negative values.
