23 · Reservation Expiration

PR: Expire reservations at their deadline

Summary

An inventory hold should be released when its deadline is reached. Review how the service transitions pending reservations while preserving active or completed records.

Input and output

expire_reservations(reservations, now) accepts records with an ID, status, and ISO 8601 expiration time. It returns copied records and IDs transitioned during this call.

Expected behavior and invariants

  • Only records with status pending can transition to expired.
  • A reservation expires when its timestamp is less than or equal to now.
  • Return newly expired IDs in input order and do not mutate caller records.
  • Reject malformed records and comparisons mixing timezone-aware and naive datetimes.

Review task

Check the exact-deadline boundary, confirmed and already-expired records, input mutation, order of IDs, and timezone compatibility.
