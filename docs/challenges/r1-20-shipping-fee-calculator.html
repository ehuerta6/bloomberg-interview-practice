PR: Calculate shipping by zone and weight

Summary

A store wants predictable parcel charges across shipping zones. Review the free-shipping cutoff and arithmetic used to price a shipment.

Input and output

calculate_shipping(order, rates) receives an order with a zone, merchandise subtotal, and weight in kilograms, plus per-zone base, per-kilogram, and free-shipping threshold values. It returns a fee rounded to cents.

Expected behavior and invariants

  • Reject unknown zones, missing fields, negative values, and nonnumeric amounts with ValueError.
  • A subtotal equal to or above the zone threshold receives free shipping regardless of weight.
  • Otherwise charge the base amount plus weight times the per-kilogram rate, rounding only the final fee to two decimal places.
  • Do not mutate the order or rate table.

Review task

Compare just-below, exactly-at, and above-threshold orders; include fractional weights and invalid quantities.
